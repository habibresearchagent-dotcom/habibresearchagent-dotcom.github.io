Neo Axis privacy policy

Neo Axis is a neonatal reference and study app for clinicians. Effective 25 November 2026.

In short: Neo Axis does not collect information about patients. If you make an account, it keeps the details you enter about yourself so that your schedule, pay entries, calendar, favourites and study progress follow you between phones. It does not sell, rent or share that information, shows no advertising, and contains no tracking or analytics.

Who runs Neo Axis

Neo Axis is made by Dr Mohamed Habib, Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. Contact: mhabib3@hsc.mb.ca, +1 431 998 8002.

Patient information

Neo Axis is built so that no patient information is entered into it or sent from it. It has no field for a patient's name, health number, record number, address or a family's contact details. Values used for a calculation (for example weight, gestation and age) are held in the phone's memory for the moment only and are cleared after 30 minutes without use, when the app closes, or when you reset them. They are never sent to the account server. Do not type anything that identifies a patient or family into free-text boxes.

Using the app without an account

Without an account, nothing leaves your phone except when you open a web link yourself. Settings, favourites and study progress stay in the app's storage on the phone. The app is excluded from Android cloud backup and from phone-to-phone transfer.

What an account keeps

  • The details you enter when you sign up: name, email address, mobile number, country, role in neonatology, date of birth, gender and (optionally) workplace.
  • The data you choose to keep in your account: your work schedule, shift times, hospitals, pay entries, calendar items, procedure log (types and dates, no patient details), favourites, guideline settings and study progress.
  • Your password only as a salted, repeatedly hashed value; the password itself is never stored. Sign-in tokens are stored only as a hash.
  • Your promo code or group, which decides which parts and guideline packs you can open, and the date you last signed in.

Sign in with Google

If you choose Sign in with Google, Google shares your email address, your name and the fact that Google has verified the email with Neo Axis. The account server checks this with Google once at sign-in and then signs you in to your Neo Axis account, which holds the same information as an email account. Neo Axis does not receive your Google password and does not ask for access to your Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

Emails we send

Sign-up and password-reset codes are sent to your address from habibresearchagent@gmail.com under the name "Neo Axis". These are the only emails the app sends. Codes work for 15 minutes and are stored only as a hash until used.

Where the data is kept

Account data is held in a Google Sheets spreadsheet and handled by a Google Apps Script program, both in the Neo Axis Google account (habibresearchagent@gmail.com). Google stores and processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy, and it may be stored outside Canada. Only the app's owner and the administrators he appoints can see members' account details, in order to manage access.

Sharing

Neo Axis does not sell, rent or trade your information and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. It may be disclosed only if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your account data is kept while your account exists. On the phone, Admin › My data deletes local data by kind, and signing out stops syncing. To have your account and all its data deleted from the server, email mhabib3@hsc.mb.ca from the address you signed up with; it will be deleted within 30 days.

Security

Connections to the account server use HTTPS. Sign-in locks for 15 minutes after five wrong passwords, and a sign-in lasts at most 60 days. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Who the app is for

Neo Axis is for health professionals and trainees. It is not meant for children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

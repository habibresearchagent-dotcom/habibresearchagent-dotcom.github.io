Neo Axis

From Winnipeg to the World

Neo Axis is an Android app for neonatal clinicians and trainees: emergency drug and equipment values by weight and gestation, a resuscitation timer with Apgar, step-by-step algorithms and checklists from the guidelines each member chooses (HSC Winnipeg policies, CPS, AAP, NICE, West Midlands, Queensland), daily-round plans, consultations, study questions and personal admin tools.

It is a reference and study tool. It is not clinically approved and does not replace clinical judgment or local policy. It does not collect patient information.
